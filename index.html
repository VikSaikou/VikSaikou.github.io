This portfolio documents hands-on security scenarios related to user onboarding, authentication methods, privileged access, auditability, access reviews, and small-business security operations.
My goal is to show how identity controls can reduce business risk, improve accountability, and make security processes easier to understand for the people who use, manage, or audit them.

  • Focus: Microsoft Entra ID, identity controls, access management
  • Interests: IAM, AI governance, security UX, small business cybersecurity
  • Goal: Document security controls in a way that is practical, clear, and auditable
